# Product Roadmap

## Purpose

The Product Roadmap describes the long-term direction of the Secure Health Identity & Access System (SHIAS).

Rather than committing to fixed dates, it organizes development into product releases based on business outcomes and customer value.

The roadmap provides a shared understanding of where the product is heading while allowing the Product Backlog to evolve through Agile planning.

---

# Product Vision

Create a secure, consent-driven health identity platform that enables trusted interoperability between healthcare providers without centralizing clinical medical records.

SHIAS empowers patients to control access to their healthcare information while enabling providers to securely discover and retrieve medical records directly from the originating systems.

---

# Prioritization Principles

Roadmap priorities are guided by the following principles:

1. Patient safety and privacy
2. Security
3. Regulatory compliance
4. Core business value
5. User experience
6. Operational excellence
7. Platform scalability

Lower-priority features should not delay delivery of high-value functionality.

---

# Roadmap Review

The Product Roadmap is reviewed:

- At the beginning of each release
- After major stakeholder feedback
- Following significant architectural changes
- During product strategy reviews

Changes to the roadmap should be driven by validated business needs rather than short-term implementation details.

---

# Success Indicators

Progress toward the roadmap is measured through:

- Completion of release objectives
- Stakeholder feedback
- Sprint outcomes
- Product adoption
- System reliability
- Security posture

Detailed metrics are tracked separately from this roadmap.
